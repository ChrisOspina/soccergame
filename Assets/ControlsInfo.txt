CONTROLS

MOVEMENT
Move ............... W A S D / Arrow Keys
Camera ............. Mouse

ON THE BALL
Shoot .............. Left Click / Space
Pass ............... Right Click

DEFENDING
Tackle ............. Left Click / Space (near the ball carrier)
Call for Pass ...... Right Click (when a teammate has the ball)
Switch Player ...... Tab

GAME
Pause / Resume ..... P / Esc
Mute / Unmute ...... M
Restart ............ R (when the match is over)

HOW TO WIN
Matches last 3 minutes.
First team to 5 goals wins.
If time runs out, the team with the most goals wins.
Level scores at full time end in a draw.

TIPS
Tackle from the front - tackles from behind usually fail.
A missed tackle leaves you frozen for a moment, so time it well.
